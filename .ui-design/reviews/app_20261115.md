# Design Review: Scandal Tracker web app (`app/`)

**Review ID:** app_20261115
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15
**Target:** `app/index.html`, `app/app.js`, `app/styles.css`
**Focus:** Visual design, usability, interaction, navigation (responsive: desktop + phone)
**Status:** All 20 suggestions and the three lower-priority notes were implemented on branch `ux/audit-20-fixes` (2026-11-15). See "Navigation and interaction" in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
**Method:** Code read plus a walk-through of every view in a browser at desktop width and at 375px, keyboard and drawer checks.

## Summary

The visual language is calm and credible, and the timeline is a good signature view. The main problems for a first-time visitor are orientation and wayfinding: the landing timeline is about 5,800px tall (127 company rows, 114 of them with a single case), the year header and legend scroll away, filters behave differently in each view and are lost on reload, and the detail drawer does not behave like a modal (phone Back leaves the view, focus escapes, the page scrolls behind it). There is also one CSS bug that silently drops a style.

**Suggestions:** 20 (6 quick fixes, 7 navigation, 4 filtering, 3 trust and learning)

---

## A. Quick fixes (small changes, big effect)

### 1. A stray `}` drops the "Prominent employee" tag style
**Location:** `app/styles.css:140` · **Category:** Visual (bug)
The lone `}` makes the browser treat `} .ptag` as an invalid selector, so the `.ptag` rule is discarded (confirmed in the browser: no `.ptag` rule is loaded). The tag in the Records table and drawer renders as plain grey text after a `·`.
**Fix:** delete line 140.

### 2. Phone Back button should close the drawer, not leave the view
**Location:** `app/app.js:167`, `app/app.js:195`
`openDrawer` uses `history.replaceState`, so opening a case adds no history entry. On a phone, pressing Back after reading a case jumps to the previous tab or leaves the site.
**Fix:** `pushState` when the drawer opens from a click; on close, `history.back()` if we pushed, otherwise `replaceState`. Handle `popstate` to close.

### 3. Make the drawer a real modal
**Location:** `app/app.js:190`
`role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` is set, but `main` is not inert, focus can Tab out behind the backdrop, and the page scrolls under the drawer.
**Fix:** on open set `main.inert = true` and `document.body.style.overflow = 'hidden'`; undo both on close. (Or switch to `<dialog>` with `showModal()`, which does all three.)

### 4. Dots are 9×9px tap targets
**Location:** `app/styles.css:74`, `app/app.js:72`
Every case on the landing view is a 9px button. That is well under the 24px minimum (WCAG 2.5.8), and it is hard to hit on a phone.
**Fix:** keep the 9px visual, enlarge the hit area:
```css
button.dot{position:relative}
button.dot::before{content:"";position:absolute;inset:-8px}
```

### 5. Keep navigation reachable on phones
**Location:** `app/styles.css:161`
The header is `position:static` below 640px, so on a ~6,000px timeline the tabs scroll away and there is no way back but scrolling.
**Fix:** keep it sticky with a compact single-row header (brand dot + tabs), or add a floating "Back to top" button after one screen of scroll.

### 6. Answer "what is this and what's new?" on the first screen
**Location:** `app/app.js:9-12`, `app/index.html:28-35`
The headline stats are "Companies" and "Lost their role". The most basic number, total cases (147), is missing, and the Calendar repeats the same two stats.
**Fix:** lead with **Cases**, then Companies, then Lost their role. Add a "Latest" strip of the 3–5 most recently disclosed cases as small cards under the intro (name, company, month, tier). It gives a new visitor an obvious first click, and gives returning visitors a reason to come back.

## B. Navigation and orientation

### 7. Sticky year header and legend on the timeline
**Location:** `app/app.js:64-68`, `app/index.html:51-56`
After the first screen you cannot tell which column is which year, and the shape legend sits below all 127 rows.
**Fix:** make the two `.tl-row.hd` rows `position:sticky; top:<header height>` inside the card, and move the legend into the toolbar or directly above the card.

### 8. Rows with no visible dots are confusing
**Location:** `app/app.js:70-74`, `app/styles.css:181`
Rows are sorted by all-time count, so on a phone (8-year window) Alphabet, CBS, Save the Children and others show a total of 2–3 but no dots. The "+n‹" hint that explains this is hidden on phones and is tiny on desktop.
**Fix:** either sort by count *within the window* and drop rows with nothing in it (with a "N more companies have earlier cases" line), or turn "+n‹" into a visible chip, such as "2 earlier ‹", that moves the window back to show them.

### 9. Tame the 127-row landing list
**Location:** `app/app.js:55-62`
The landing page is ~7.5 screens tall and 90% of rows hold one case.
**Fix (pick one or combine):** a "Find a company" input in the toolbar that filters rows; show the top 25 by count with "Show all 127 companies"; or a "Group by sector" toggle with collapsible groups.

### 10. Truncated company names
**Location:** `app/styles.css:58`
47 of 127 names are cut off at desktop width ("21st Century Fox (Fox N", "Comcast (NBCUniversa"), and the `title` tooltip doesn't help on touch.
**Fix:** add an optional `short_name` to `companies.json` for the timeline (keep the full name in the drawer and Records), or allow two lines with `-webkit-line-clamp:2`.

### 11. Make company names clickable
**Location:** `app/app.js:73`, `app/app.js:125`
A company is the natural unit people come to look up, but its name is inert everywhere.
**Fix:** clicking a company (timeline row label, Records cell, drawer meta line) opens Records filtered to that company (`#records?company=fox`), with a removable "Company: 21st Century Fox ×" chip. A light company page can come later.

### 12. Previous / next inside the drawer
**Location:** `app/app.js:162-192`
To read several cases you close, find the next dot, open, close again.
**Fix:** add "‹ Prev · 3 of 147 · Next ›" at the drawer's top, stepping through the current filtered and sorted list, with ←/→ keys. When the same person has several records (e.g. Nvidia, 3 records), add "Other records about this person" links.

### 13. One search that works from any view
**Location:** `app/index.html:100-102`
Search exists only on Records. Someone arriving with a name in mind has to know to switch tabs first.
**Fix:** put the search field (or a search icon on phones) in the header. Typing shows a small results list of people and companies; Enter goes to Records with the query applied. Support the `/` shortcut to focus it.

## C. Filtering and state

### 14. One filter state, shared by every view
**Location:** `app/app.js:14`, `app/app.js:82`, `app/index.html:40-111`
Each view has a different set of controls: Scope is only on Timeline, Unconfirmed is on Timeline and Calendar but stored separately (`showU` vs `calU`), and Records has neither. Switching tabs silently changes what you are looking at.
**Fix:** one shared filter bar (Type, Scope, Evidence, Prominent) with the same state in every view, plus a "Filters (2) · Reset" summary when anything is off its default.

### 15. Put filters and search in the URL
**Location:** `app/app.js:200-215`
Only `#view/id` is stored. A search or filter is lost on reload and can't be shared ("all misconduct cases at Global 500 companies").
**Fix:** serialize to the hash, e.g. `#records?type=misconduct&scope=Global%20500&q=ceo`, and parse it in `show()`.

### 16. Sort and filter the Records table
**Location:** `app/index.html:105`, `app/app.js:132-141`
The table is fixed to newest-first with free-text search only.
**Fix:** sortable column headers (Disclosed, Company, Evidence) with `aria-sort`; quick filters for Evidence tier and "Lost their role: Yes/No". Show "Showing 23 of 147" whenever a filter is active.

### 17. Helpful empty states
**Location:** `app/app.js:76`, `app/app.js:138`
"No records match 'x'." doesn't tell people that an unchecked filter (e.g. Prominent employees) may be hiding results.
**Fix:** "No cases match 'x' with the current filters. [Clear search] [Reset filters]", and list which filters are active.

## D. Trust and learning

### 18. Explain evidence tiers where they appear
**Location:** `app/app.js:129`, `app/app.js:182`, `app/app.js:151`
"Company confirmed", "Court ruling", "Unconfirmed" are central to how much to trust a case, but their meaning lives only in the About page, with no anchors.
**Fix:** give About sections `id`s (`#about/evidence`) and a short table of contents; make the tier pill in the drawer and the legend link to that anchor, or open a small popover with the one-line definition from the methodology table.

### 19. Show freshness, sources and a way to report errors
**Location:** `app/app.js:176-189`, `app/index.html` (no footer)
For a dataset about real people, readers need to see how current a record is and how to correct it. `verified_on` is in every record but never shown, and there is no footer.
**Fix:** in the drawer, add "Verified Nov 13, 2026", a "Copy link" button, and the archive link when `archive_url` exists. Add a site footer with "Data last updated <max verified_on>", a corrections or contact link, and "Download data (JSON/CSV)".

### 20. Calendar: bring the selected month into view
**Location:** `app/app.js:110-117`
Clicking a month in the 2010 row renders its case list below the entire grid (~30 rows down), so it looks like nothing happened.
**Fix:** after rendering, `monthList.scrollIntoView({behavior:'smooth', block:'start'})`, and add a "× Close" button. Alternatively, open the month's cases in the existing drawer as a list.

## Also noted (lower priority)
- No dark mode. Colors are tokens in CSS, but the heat map writes `rgba(200,65,43,…)` and `#fff` inline from JS (`app/app.js:89`, `:101`). Move those to tokens first.
- No loading state: the page shows empty headings until all four fetches finish. A simple skeleton or "Loading cases…" line would help on slow connections.
- Segmented controls are plain buttons with `aria-pressed`, which is fine, but the type control re-renders on every `show()`, so keyboard focus is lost after picking a type (`app/app.js:209`).

## Positive observations
- A restrained, editorial look: good type pairing, plenty of whitespace, and an accent color used sparingly.
- Shape *and* color encode the case type, so it doesn't rely on color alone.
- The phone layout reflows Records into readable cards, and the timeline narrows its year window on small screens.
- Good basics: skip link, `aria-live` on counts, `focus-visible` outlines, Escape closes the drawer and focus returns to the trigger.
- Deep links to a case (`#timeline/92`) already work.

## Next steps
1. Ship the quick fixes (1–6), all small and contained.
2. Unify filter state and put it in the URL (14, 15). Most navigation work builds on this.
3. Rework the landing timeline's length and orientation (7–10).
